# Customer notices

Send these from the **buyer's** systems after closing. Replace the brackets.

## 1. Email to product-update subscribers

Send with `npm run admin -- send-update "<subject>" notice.txt`, which adds the unsubscribe link and headers automatically.

**Subject:** Crewdeck has a new owner

> Hello,
>
> You're receiving this because you asked for Crewdeck product updates. On [DATE], [BUYER LEGAL NAME] took over Crewdeck from [SELLER LEGAL NAME].
>
> **What this means for you:**
> - The app keeps working as before. Your agents and conversations stay on your device; neither company ever held them.
> - If you subscribe to Crewdeck Pro, your subscription continues on the same terms, and you don't need to do anything.
> - Your email address and the record of your consent have moved to [BUYER LEGAL NAME], who will use them only to send product updates, as described in our Privacy Policy: [WEBSITE]/privacy.html
>
> If you'd rather not hear from us, use the unsubscribe link below and we'll delete your address straight away.
>
> [BUYER LEGAL NAME], [ADDRESS]

## 2. Website banner (first 30 days)

> Crewdeck is now run by [BUYER LEGAL NAME]. Your subscription and data are unaffected. [Read what's changed](/privacy.html)

## 3. App update release notes

> Crewdeck is now provided by [BUYER LEGAL NAME]. We've updated our Terms and Privacy Policy to show the new owner's details, and you'll be asked to review them. Nothing about how your data is stored has changed, and Crewdeck Pro subscriptions continue as before.
