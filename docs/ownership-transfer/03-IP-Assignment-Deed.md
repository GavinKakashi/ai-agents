# Deed of Assignment of Intellectual Property (template)

> Template only. Some jurisdictions require a deed to be witnessed or notarised, or need a separate form to record a trademark assignment. Take legal advice.

**This Deed** is made on [DATE]

**by** [SELLER LEGAL NAME] of [ADDRESS] (the **Assignor**)

**in favour of** [BUYER LEGAL NAME] of [ADDRESS] (the **Assignee**).

## Background

The Assignor has agreed to sell the Crewdeck business to the Assignee under an Asset Purchase Agreement dated [DATE] (the **Agreement**).

## 1. Assignment

In consideration of the Price under the Agreement (receipt of which the Assignor acknowledges), the Assignor assigns to the Assignee, with full title guarantee, all of its right, title and interest worldwide in:

(a) the copyright and all other rights in the Crewdeck software, including its source code, object code, documentation, scripts, website and legal texts;

(b) the Crewdeck name and logo, and any trademarks, applications and registrations for them, including [TRADEMARK NUMBERS], together with the goodwill attached to them;

(c) the app artwork, icons, screenshots and other design works;

(d) all domain names listed in Schedule 1 of the Agreement;

(e) the right to sue for and keep damages for past infringements of any of the above,

for the full term of those rights and any renewals and extensions (the **Assigned IP**).

## 2. Excluded

Open-source software components, which remain subject to their own licences, are not assigned and are not owned by the Assignor.

## 3. Moral rights

To the extent permitted by law, the Assignor waives, and will procure that the individual authors waive, any moral rights in the Assigned IP.

## 4. Further assurance

The Assignor will, at the Assignee's request and cost, sign any documents and do anything reasonably necessary to record or perfect the Assignee's ownership of the Assigned IP, including filings with trademark offices and domain registrars.
