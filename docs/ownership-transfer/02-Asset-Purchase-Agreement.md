## 1. Definitions

- **Business:** the Crewdeck mobile app, website and related service as carried on by the Seller.
- **Transferred Assets:** the assets listed in Schedule 1 (Asset Inventory).
- **Excluded Assets:** the assets listed in Part E of Schedule 1.
- **Closing:** completion of the sale under clause 5.
- **Transfer Date:** the date on which the Apple and Google app transfers complete, or [DATE], whichever is later.
- **Escrow Agent:** [ESCROW SERVICE].

## 2. Sale and purchase

2.1 The Seller sells and the Buyer buys the Transferred Assets, free from any charges, liens or third-party claims, with effect from Closing.

2.2 The Buyer does not take on any liabilities of the Seller except obligations arising after the Transfer Date under customer subscriptions and source-code licences listed in Schedule 1, Part D.

## 3. Price

3.1 The purchase price is **[AMOUNT AND CURRENCY]** (the **Price**), [inclusive / exclusive] of [VAT / sales taxes].

3.2 The Buyer pays the Price into escrow with the Escrow Agent within [5] business days of signing. The Escrow Agent releases it to the Seller when both parties have signed the Closing Handover Certificate.

3.3 [Optional earn-out: an additional [X]% of net subscription revenue for [12] months after Closing, paid quarterly, capped at [AMOUNT].]

3.4 Store proceeds for sales before the Transfer Date belong to the Seller. Store proceeds for sales from the Transfer Date belong to the Buyer. If either party receives money belonging to the other, it pays that money over within [30] days.

## 4. Due diligence

The Buyer has had the opportunity to review the source code, the Seller Disclosure Schedule (Schedule 2) and the store reports. The Seller has answered the Buyer's questions truthfully.

## 5. Closing and handover

5.1 The parties will complete the steps in the Account Transfer Guide in the order set out there, starting within [5] business days after the Price is in escrow.

5.2 At Closing the Seller delivers:
(a) the source code (repository transfer and a complete archive);
(b) the signed IP Assignment Deed;
(c) completed transfers of the iOS and Android apps, including their subscription products;
(d) the domain, website hosting and email accounts, or working forwarding during the transition period;
(e) the mailing list, consent records and suppression list;
(f) all store sales reports up to the Transfer Date;
(g) all passwords, keys and credentials needed to operate the Transferred Assets, delivered securely and not by plain email.

5.3 Closing takes place when both parties sign the Closing Handover Certificate (Schedule 3).

## 6. Seller's warranties

The Seller warrants that, at signing and at Closing:

6.1 it owns the Transferred Assets (other than open-source components) and has the right to sell them;

6.2 the app's original code, artwork and texts were created by or for the Seller, and their creators have assigned their rights to the Seller;

6.3 open-source components are used in line with their licences, as listed in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`;

6.4 it has no knowledge of any claim that the Business infringes anyone's rights;

6.5 the information in the Seller Disclosure Schedule, including revenue and subscriber figures, is true and not misleading;

6.6 personal data in the Transferred Assets was collected in line with the privacy policy then in force, and the mailing list contains only people who gave valid, recorded consent;

6.7 there are no outstanding store policy strikes, takedown notices or unresolved refund disputes, except as disclosed.

## 7. Buyer's obligations

7.1 The Buyer will honour existing customer subscriptions under the terms in force at the Transfer Date, and will not reduce subscribers' rights because of the transfer.

7.2 The Buyer will process transferred personal data only as the privacy policy describes, or after notifying people of changes and, where required, obtaining their consent.

7.3 The Buyer will publish updated legal pages naming itself as the provider within [14] days of Closing (see `npm run transfer`).

## 8. Transition services

The Seller will provide up to [NUMBER] hours of reasonable technical and handover support during the [30/60/90] days after Closing, by [email / video call], at [no extra cost / RATE per hour].

## 9. Restrictive covenants (optional)

## 10. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the terms of this Agreement and the other's confidential information private, except as required by law or to its professional advisers.

## 11. Liability

11.1 The Seller's total liability under the warranties is limited to [the Price / AMOUNT], and claims must be notified within [12/24] months of Closing.

11.2 Neither party limits liability for fraud, or for anything else that cannot be limited by law.

## 12. Taxes and costs

Each party pays its own legal and advisory costs. [Escrow fees are split equally.] Each party is responsible for its own taxes arising from the sale.

## 13. Further assurance

Each party will sign any further documents and take any steps reasonably needed to give full effect to this Agreement, including store, registrar and trademark-office forms.

## 14. General

14.1 This Agreement and its schedules are the entire agreement between the parties about the sale.

14.2 Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.

14.3 This Agreement is governed by [GOVERNING LAW], and the courts of [JURISDICTION] have exclusive jurisdiction.
