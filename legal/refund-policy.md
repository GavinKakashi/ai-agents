## Purchases made through the App Store or Google Play

Apple and Google process payments for purchases made in their stores, so refunds for those purchases are handled by them under their own policies:

- **Apple App Store:** request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
- **Google Play:** request a refund from your Google Play order history, or via Google Play Help.

If a store refuses a refund and you believe the purchase was faulty or not as described, contact us at {{supportEmail}} and we will help.

## Purchases made directly from us (including source-code licences)

- **14-day refund window.** You can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase, for any reason, provided you have not downloaded or accessed the digital content.
- **After download.** If you ask us to provide digital content immediately and you acknowledge at checkout that this ends your 14-day cancellation right, the right to cancel ends once the download starts. This does not affect your legal rights if the content is faulty or not as described — in that case we will repair it, replace it or refund you, as the law requires.
- **How to request a refund.** Email {{supportEmail}} with your order number. We do not require you to give a reason.
- **Timing and method.** We will refund you to the original payment method within 14 days of approving your request. We do not charge any fee for refunds.

## AI provider charges

Charges for AI usage are billed by Anthropic to your own Anthropic account. We do not receive these payments and cannot refund them. Contact Anthropic about any usage charge.
