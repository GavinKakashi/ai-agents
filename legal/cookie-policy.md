# Cookie Policy

**Effective date:** {{effectiveDate}} · **Version:** {{policyVersion}}

This policy explains how {{appName}} uses cookies and similar technologies such as browser local storage.

## The mobile app

The iOS and Android apps do not use cookies. They store your data on your device so that the app works (see the Privacy Policy). They contain no tracking or advertising technology.

## The web version of the app

The web version stores your agents, conversations, settings and consent record in your browser's local storage, so that the app works. This is strictly necessary for the service you asked for, so it does not need consent, but you can clear it at any time with **Settings → Privacy center → Delete all data**, or by clearing your browser's site data. Your API key is never written to browser storage on the web; it is kept in memory only.

## The website ({{websiteUrl}})

| Name | Type | Category | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cd_consent` | Local storage | Strictly necessary | Remembers your cookie choices so we don't ask again | 12 months, then we ask again |

**By default the website sets no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising scripts.** The cookie banner lets you allow or refuse optional categories. Optional categories stay off unless you choose to turn them on, and refusing is as easy as accepting.

If we ever add an optional technology (for example privacy-friendly analytics), we will list it in this table before it is used, and it will run only if you opt in.

## Changing your choice

Use the **Cookie settings** link in the footer of every page on the website to change or withdraw your choice at any time. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it.
