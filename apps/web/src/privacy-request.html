Make a privacy request

Use this form to exercise your data protection rights, such as access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent. We'll reply within one month (45 days for California residents).

Your data in the app is not held by us. Agents, conversations and settings are stored only on your device. To export or delete them, open the app and go to Settings → Privacy center.

This form covers what we do hold: product-update subscriptions and earlier privacy requests. We only use the details you enter here to handle your request. You can also email {{privacyEmail}}.
